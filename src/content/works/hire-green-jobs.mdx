## Project Snapshot and Contribution

HiRE is a public-facing green jobs platform developed for the Society of Renewable Energy (SRE), connecting people with sustainability careers, learning resources, and events.

**My contribution:** Frontend implementation, content-driven page structure, and support for production deployment. I worked with a UI/UX designer and backend engineers.


## Product Challenge

Job listings, learning resources, and events needed clear page layouts that worked on both phones and desktop screens.
