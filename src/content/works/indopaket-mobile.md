## UX Analysis

### Heuristic Evaluation

**Recognition Rather Than Recall** *(Nielsen's Heuristic #6)*
On the home screen, the tracking search bar sits next to two icons whose purpose isn't immediately distinguishable at a glance, requiring users to guess or recall which one does what.
