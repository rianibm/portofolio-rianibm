## A little context

Member+ Reward Draw CMS is an internal module for running roulette-style reward draws during live campaigns. It serves both campaign operators and event audiences.

**My part:** UX design and frontend implementation, with ongoing ownership across campaign cycles from July 2025 through the April 2026 release.

I’ve simplified a few details here to keep internal information private.


## Where things got tricky

Operators need to know which stage the draw is in and what to do next. Viewers need to read the result clearly, including when watching the broadcast on a phone.

## What I built

The module was used across several live campaigns. I continued updating the preparation screens and broadcast views through the April 2026 release.


## What stayed with me

I had to think about two people at once: the operator running the draw and the viewer watching it. A screen that worked for the operator still needed readable text and enough time for the audience to follow the result.
