## A little context

IDDR brings community activities and learning resources into a single platform. The public website includes a digital store, a vault of webinar recordings, events, community registration, and service enquiries.

**My part:** Web development for the public platform and admin workspace, alongside the data foundation for later integration.

## Two sides of the same platform

Members need a clear way to find an event, recording, or product. The team needs a way to review applications, maintain content, and follow up on orders and service requests. I worked on both experiences so the public pages and administrative tasks could be developed together.

## Building the admin workflows

The admin prototype includes search, filters, pagination, content editors, application review, member management, and order details. Browser persistence makes these workflows interactive during development, while role-specific views help explain who handles each task.

The project also includes a Prisma database foundation. The browser demo and database remain separate at this stage; the prototype does not establish production authentication or shared persistence.

## Where it stands

IDDR is **in development**. The public interfaces and admin prototype are implemented, while production authentication, payments, paid-content access, and transactional email remain integration work.

The current result is a working interface foundation for reviewing the flows before those services are connected. No launch or business impact is claimed here.
