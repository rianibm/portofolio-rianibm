## A little context

Dashboard Member Plus is a supporting seller platform within the Indopaket ecosystem, where sellers can check referrals, membership, transaction history, and account details.

**My part:** Frontend architecture and authentication integration using Next.js and React.

I’ve simplified a few details here to keep internal information private.


## Where things got tricky

The overview needed to show account activity at a glance, with transaction records and profile details on separate pages.
